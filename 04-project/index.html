Info about the NFL

The National Football League (NFL) is the premier professional American football league in the world, featuring 32 teams divided into the American Football Conference (AFC) and National Football Conference (NFC). Each conference has 16 teams, organized into four divisions: East, North, South, and West. The NFL season typically runs from September through February, ending with the Super Bowl, one of the biggest sporting events in the world. With passionate fans and legendary players make the game exciting. The NFL has become a major part of American sports and culture.

After 18 weeks of Football, 14 Teams compete in the Playoffs for a chance to play in the Superbowl. 7 teams come from each conference with the division winners guaranteed to make it, While the remaining spots are filled based on record.
