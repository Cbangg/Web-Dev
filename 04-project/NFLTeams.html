NFL Teams and Locations

There is two conferences in the National Football League and 16 teams are split into either the American Football Conference (AFC) or The National Football League (NFC)

Here are the AFC Teams AFC East: Buffalo Bills, Miami Dolphins, New England Patriots and The New York Jets. AFC North: Baltimore Ravens, Cincinnati Bengals, Cleveland Browns and The Pittsburgh Steelers. AFC South: Houston Texans, Indianapolis Colts, Jacksonville Jaguars and The Tennessee Titans. AFC West: Denver Broncos, Kansas City Chiefs, Las Vegas Raiders and The Los Angeles Chargers.

Here are the NFC Teams: NFC East: Dallas Cowboys, New York Giants, Philadelphia Eagles and The Washington Commanders. NFC North: Chicago Bears, Detroit Lions, Green Bay Packers and The Minnesota Vikings. NFC South: Atlanta Falcons, Carolina Panthers, New Orleans Saints and The Tampa Bay Buccaneers. NFC West: Arizona Cardinals, Los Angeles Rams, San Francisco 49ers and The Seattle Seahawks
